**AI Trust & Verification — Interactive HCI Research Prototype** · 2026
React · TypeScript · Human–AI Interaction
[Code](https://github.com/ivyhongzhan/ai-trust-verification) · [Demo](https://ivyhongzhan.github.io/ai-trust-verification/)

Developed an interactive research prototype extending questions raised by my study of generative-AI trust discourse across Reddit communities. Implemented a React/TypeScript workspace for manual claim review, source transparency, evidence limitations, separate trust/distrust reflections, contextual use decisions, local persistence, and portable exports. Created synthetic personal-planning and public-communication scenarios to explore contextual differences without inferring behavior from discourse labels. Specified a future randomized study comparing structured review with free-form notes, using independently assessed judgment quality and effort measures. Verified core functionality through automated tests and browser checks. AI-assisted development is disclosed; usability, reliance effects, and intervention effectiveness remain unevaluated.
